02 Scoring Method & Conversion System

1. Scale Conversion Protocol

The survey instrument includes seven rating questions. Two questions were excluded from the instructional mentor index: Syllabus Pace (Col H) is a directional metric (*Too Fast*, *Just Right*, *Too Slow*) tied to institutional syllabus planning. Test Platform (Col M) measures technical infrastructure rather than teaching capability.

The remaining five instructional questions were scored on a standardized 1 to 5 integer scale:

• Satisfaction (G): Extremely Satisfied = 5, Very Satisfied = 4, Satisfied = 3, Dissatisfied = 2, Very Dissatisfied = 1
• Clarity (I): Extremely clear = 5, Very Clear = 4, Moderately Clear = 3, Slightly Clear = 2, Not Clear at All = 1
• Mentor Rating (J): Excellent = 5, Very Good = 4, Good = 3, Needs Improvement = 2, Needs Significant Improvement = 1
• Engagement (K): Highly Engaging = 5, Very Engaging = 4, Moderately Engaging = 3, Slightly Engaging = 2, Not Engaging = 1
• Class Quality (L): Excellent = 5, Very Good = 4, Good = 3, Needs Improvement = 2, Needs Significant Improvement = 1

2. Overall Score Formulation

For each mentor, the average score across each question was computed:
Average(Q) = Σ(Scores in Q) / n
The composite Overall Score is calculated as the unweighted arithmetic mean of the five question averages:
Overall Score = [Average(G) + Average(I) + Average(J) + Average(K) + Average(L)] / 5
Because every respondent completed all five questions with zero missing entries, this is mathematically identical to Total Points Earned / (n × 5).

04 Key Findings & Next Steps for Leadership
Key Program Strengths
  • Mentor Interpersonal Connection (Cohort Avg: 4.12/5.00): Across all mentors, personal ratings exceed other categories. Over 80% rate mentors as Very Good or Excellent.
  • Syllabus Pacing Balance: 72.7% rate the pace as Just Right, indicating well-sequenced curriculum delivery.
  • Benchmark Exemplar (Mentor E): Achieved 4.41 overall with high ratings in Mentor Commitment (4.60) and Class Quality (4.47), driven by practical competitive coding problems.
Key Areas for Improvement
  • Explanation Clarity (Cohort Avg: 3.85/5.00): Lowest scoring category across the survey. Students request visual walkthroughs for complex data structures.
  • Mentor A Section 2 Divergence: Mentor A scored 3.89 in Section 1, but dropped to 3.61 in Section 2, driven by low satisfaction (3.54) and classroom management friction.
  • CodeFri Test Platform Stability: 16% negative ratings. Over 35 qualitative remarks reported browser freezing and screen sharing dropouts during tests.
Recommended Next Steps for Academic Leadership
  1. CodeFri Browser Technical Optimization: Coordinate with the technology infrastructure team to address memory leaks and screen sharing stability in the CodeFri assessment environment prior to final examinations.
  2. Pedagogical Alignment for Mentor A (Section 2): Pair Mentor A with Mentor E to review interactive coding demonstrations and structured doubt resolution strategies.
  3. Standardized Practice Framework: Integrate structured problem-solving sessions balancing exam format requirements (NeoColab) with practical LeetCode-style problem solving.
  4. Standardized Survey Administration: Implement dedicated 10-minute in-class survey completion buffers to ensure smaller cohorts (such as Mentor H and Mentor G) attain representative response volumes ($n \ge 15$).
